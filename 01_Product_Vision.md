# Product Vision Document 

## Product Vision Statement
"Develop a secure and user-friendly Student Record and Academic Management Platform that enables educational institutions to efficiently manage student records, attendance, academic performance, course registration, and reporting through a scalable web-based application.

## Core Objectives
* Streamline academic administrative processes into a single unified platform.
* Provide role-based access for students, faculty, and administrative staff.
* Deliver real-time tracking for attendance, marks, and academic performance.

## Key Stakeholders & Target Users
* **Students**: View profiles, course enrollments, attendance records, and academic results.
* **Faculty Members**: Manage course assignments, record daily attendance, enter and update student marks.
* **Administrators**: Register students, manage courses, configure role-based security, and generate institution-wide reports.

## Measurable Success Criteria
* 99.9% uptime for core authentication and academic modules.
* Reduction of manual attendance entry errors by 80.
* Ability to generate comprehensive student/course performance reports in under 3 seconds[.

## Project Constraints
* Web-based architecture requiring secure access control.
* Strict data privacy compliance for student academic records.
